The Narrative of Weight

Every motion carries an implied mass. A bounce suggests playfulness; a slow, linear fade suggests solemnity. When these physics-based interactions are applied consistently, they create a "spatial intuition" for the user. They no longer need to think about where they are; they feel the architecture of the app through its resistance and velocity.

Extended Session: Applying Narrative Curves to Utility Interfaces

"Motion is the bridge between what the user sees and what the user understands."

— Julian Vane, 2024 Keynote

In the second half of the presentation, we look at the psychological impact of transition speed. We found that transitions under 200ms feel instantaneous, while those over 500ms begin to feel like a "journey." Choosing where to place your experience on this spectrum is the difference between a tool and an experience.
