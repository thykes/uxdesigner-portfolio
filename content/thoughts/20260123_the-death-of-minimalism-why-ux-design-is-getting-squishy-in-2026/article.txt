Title: The Death of Minimalism: Why UX Design Is Getting ‘Squishy’ in 2026

----

Intro: As the digital landscape pivots from the clinical detachment of flat design, a new era of "Tactile Surrealism" is redefining how we interact with our screens. This shift, led by a surge in organic textures and responsive depth, suggests that the internet is finally trading its cold efficiency for a more visceral, human pulse.
